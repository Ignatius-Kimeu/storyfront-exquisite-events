Where we work

Nairobi, and anywhere you can get a lorry to.

Most of what you see was built in and around Nairobi — garden venues, marquees on open country, hotel ballrooms. We travel for the rest.

If your venue is outside the city, tell us where in the first message and we will include transport and setup time in the quote rather than surprising you with it later.
